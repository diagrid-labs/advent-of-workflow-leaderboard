# Code of Conduct

Advent of Workflow is run by Diagrid and open to the Dapr community. This code of conduct applies in this repository, the challenge repository (`diagrid-labs/advent-of-workflow`), and any other space where the event is discussed.

## Our standard

We want this to be a friendly place to learn Dapr Workflow, including for people who have never used Dapr.

Expected behaviour:

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's ideas and code.
- Respect the rules and the evidence requirements. Submit your own work.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the event, or submitting fabricated screenshots or runs.
- Disrupting issues or pull requests to gain an advantage in the standings.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, comments, discussions and any other event-related communication.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close pull requests, and exclude participants from the standings and prizes for behaviour that breaks this code. Decisions are made by the event maintainers.

To report a problem, contact the organisers at: **marc@diagrid.io**
